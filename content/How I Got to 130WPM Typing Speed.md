That is a screenshot of my fastest typing speed recorded on my [MonkeyType profile.](https://monkeytype.com/profile/DrBlitzer) That is a speed of 131 words per minute (WPM). For context, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Words_per_minute), "professional typists type at speeds of 43 to 80 wpm". Table 1 of [this paper](https://www.asarif.com/pub/Arif_TIC-STH2009.pdf) shows an average WPM of 65 and 87 for 2 separate studies looking at typing speeds. It's safe to say that my typing speed is well above the average typist. So how did I get here?

It starts many years ago. I was in my early teens, I think 13 or 14, and I wanted to be a cool hacker.

- do at least 10 mins keybr everyday
- test on monkey type before and after practice
- slow down
- Aim for 100% accuracy, not high speed
- when you get a word wrong - delete the whole word and rewrite it using ctrl+backspace or option+backspace
- if you miss a day just continue the next day
- I used to practice this many years ago but recently I've realised that my typing has gotten worse. Slower and more mistakes which makes it even slower.
- keybr settings:
	- target typing speed: 100wpm
	- unlock a next key only when the previous keys are also above the target speed
	- prefer natural words
	- repeat each word = 2
	- add words to lessons = about 40%
	- daily goal = 10 minutes
