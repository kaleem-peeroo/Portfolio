I use the 3 pass method from [[1_main/How to Read a Paper|How to Read a Paper]]. In it's essence you do the following 3 passes where each pass builds upon the last:

- Pass 1
  - 5 to 10 mins
  - read the
    - title
    - abstract
    - intro
    - headings and subheadings
    - conclusion
- Pass 2
  - up to 1 hour
  - rest the entire paper while skipping details like proofs and algorithms
- Pass 3
  - up to 6 hours
  - virtually recreate the paper

At the end of the first pass you have a vague idea of what the paper is about. With this info you can decide whether its worth reading further or to be discarded. If good to continue reading then you do pass 2.
